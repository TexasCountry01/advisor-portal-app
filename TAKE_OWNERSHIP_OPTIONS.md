# Take Ownership on Pending Review Cases

**Current behavior:** The "Take Ownership" button is hidden when a case is in Pending Review status.

---

**Option A — Allow reviewer to Take Ownership of Pending Review cases**

The reviewer can click "Take Ownership" to claim the case, then proceed with the review. This adds `pending_review` as an allowed status for taking ownership.

**Option B — Reviewer reviews without taking ownership**

The reviewer was already selected during the "Submit for Review" step and has access to the case. They don't need to own it — they just review, approve/request corrections, and the original tech keeps ownership. The button stays hidden for Pending Review.
