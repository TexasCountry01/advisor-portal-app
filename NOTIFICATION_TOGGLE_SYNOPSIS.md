# Notification Self-Service Controls — Synopsis

## Overview
Members and delegates can now control their own email and in-app (portal) notifications from their **Profile** page. No technician intervention is needed.

## What Members See on Their Profile

- **My Alert Settings** — Two simple on/off toggles:
  - **Email Notifications** — Controls whether case-related emails are sent to the member
  - **In-App Notifications** — Controls whether case alerts appear in the portal notification bell
- **My Delegates** — Read-only list showing who has been assigned as a delegate on their behalf

## What Delegates See on Their Profile

- **Delegate Alert Settings** — A table listing each member they represent, with independent toggles per member:
  - **In-App** — Toggle in-app alerts for that member's cases
  - **Email** — Toggle email alerts for that member's cases
- Delegates manage their own preferences — members cannot change delegate notification settings

## How It Works

- **Email suppression**: When a member turns off email notifications, all case-related emails (hold, chat, completed) stop being sent to them. Delegate emails are controlled separately per assignment.
- **In-app suppression**: When a member turns off in-app notifications, no new alerts appear in the notification bell for their cases.
- **Delegate-level control**: Each delegate can independently silence notifications for specific members they represent without affecting other delegates.
- **Defaults**: All notifications are **enabled by default** — members and delegates opt out only if they choose to.

## Where to Access

- Log in → Click username in the top-right → **Profile**
- Alert settings appear in clearly labeled cards on the profile page
