# New Feature: Messages

**Date:** March 16, 2026  
**Status:** Ready for testing

---

## What's New

A new **"Messages"** area has been added to the portal. This gives members a way to ask general questions to the team — without needing to have a case open.

---

## For Members

- A new **"Messages"** link appears in the top navigation bar
- Click it to see all your conversations
- Click **"New Question"** to ask the team something
  - Enter a subject and your question
  - Check **"Urgent"** if it needs immediate attention
- When staff replies, a **red badge** appears on the Messages link so you know there's something new
- Click into any conversation to see the full back-and-forth and reply

---

## For Staff (Technicians / Admins)

- A new **"Messages"** link appears in the top navigation bar (with a red unread badge)
- The **Message Queue** shows all incoming questions from members
- Filter by:
  - Open / Closed
  - Assigned to Me / Unassigned
  - Urgent only
- Actions on each conversation:
  - **Claim** — assign it to yourself
  - **Take Over** — reassign from another staff member to yourself
  - **Reply** — respond to the member
  - **Close** — mark as resolved
  - **Reopen** — reopen if the member has a follow-up
- If a member replies to a closed conversation, it **automatically reopens**

---

## How It Relates to Cases

- This is **completely separate** from case discussions
- Case chat still lives on the case page — nothing has changed there
- Messages is only for general questions that aren't tied to a specific case

---

## What Hasn't Changed

- **Beta Feedback** is still available — no changes there
- All existing case workflows, notifications, and dashboards remain the same
- This is a brand-new addition, not a replacement for anything
