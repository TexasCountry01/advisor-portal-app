# ProFeds Error Reporting — Question & Answer for Review

**Question from user:** "How will the ProFeds Errors reporting work if someone says it's a ProFed error, but Tiffany clears it because it was really the advisor's error?"

---

## How It Works Today

When a member flags a modification request as a ProFeds error, the system records it and it immediately appears in the ProFeds Errors count on the Performance Dashboard and Scorecard.

Any technician, manager, or administrator can **clear** that flag — but only with a written justification. The clearance and the reason are permanently recorded in the system's audit trail.

**The problem:** When the flag is cleared, the error count goes down immediately. If the scorecard showed **3 errors** for a given week, and Tiffany later clears one, it now shows **2** — even for a week that has already passed. The metric changed retroactively.

This also affects the **Report Accuracy** percentage, which improves when an error flag is cleared.

---

## Why This Matters for Evaluations

There are two concerns:

1. **Numbers are not stable.** A manager could review Week X, see 3 errors, and come back later to find 2 — with no indication anything changed.

2. **Techs can influence their own metrics.** A technician can clear their own error flag. Even though it requires a justification and is logged, the number they are being evaluated on goes down.

For a report feeding HR evaluations, this is a problem.

---

## The Options

### Option A — Count all reported errors, regardless of outcome
Once an error is reported, it stays in the count permanently. Clearances are still allowed and recorded, but they do not lower the number. The metric answers: *"How many times did a member report a ProFeds error?"*

### Option B — Show both: reported and cleared *(Most transparent)*
The scorecard shows two numbers side by side: **Reported** (total claimed) and **Disputed** (cleared by a tech). The manager sees the full picture — how many were claimed and how many were later challenged.

### Option C — Keep current behavior
Cleared errors disappear from the count. The metric reflects only errors that are still active. Clearances are still audited but the number can go down.

---

## Recommendation

**Option A** is the simplest change and best protects the integrity of the evaluation. An error was reported — that is a fact of record regardless of who was at fault. The outcome (cleared or not) is visible on the individual case.

**Option B** adds more context for the manager but requires more screen space and explanation.

**Option C** (current behavior) is not recommended for formal evaluations because techs can lower their own metrics.
