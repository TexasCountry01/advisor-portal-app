Metric Definitions
Reports Generated — Cases where the tech clicked "Release Case" (status = completed) within the date window.
Submitted for Review — Number of times any tech submitted a case for senior review within the date window. One case can generate multiple events.
On-Time Delivery % — Completed cases where date_completed ≤ date_due, divided by total completed cases with a due date.
ProFeds Errors — Modification cases created by a member who flagged the issue as a ProFeds error, filtered by when the mod was submitted.
Avg Production Cycle Time — Average days from when the member submitted the original case to when the tech released it.
Avg Readiness Window — Average days in advance of the due date that the tech finished. Positive = early; negative = late.
Report Accuracy — Percentage of completed cases that have no ProFeds error flag. Note: errors are member self-reported and may appear days or weeks after completion.
